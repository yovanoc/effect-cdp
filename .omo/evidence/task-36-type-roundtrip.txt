      For each char: Input.dispatchKeyEvent({type:"keyDown",text:c})
                  then Input.dispatchKeyEvent({type:"keyUp",text:c})

  - fill(cdp, sessionId, nodeId, value): Effect.fnUntraced
      DOM.focus({nodeId}) then Runtime.evaluate sets document.activeElement.value
      and dispatches 'input'/'change' events. Value is escaped for template literal.
      Decodes result as Schema.Null.

  - press(cdp, sessionId, key): Effect.fnUntraced
      Looks up key in KEY_MAP (Enter, Tab, Escape, ArrowUp/Down/Left/Right).
      Dispatches keyDown then keyUp with key/code/windowsVirtualKeyCode.
      Unsupported key -> yield* new CdpProtocolError({code:-1, message:"Unsupported key: <k>", method:"Input.dispatchKeyEvent"})
